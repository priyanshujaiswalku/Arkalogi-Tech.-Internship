💡 Desk Alignment & Mathematical Formulations (Futures First & Axxela Evaluators)

1. Analytical Greeks (Black-Scholes-Merton):
• Delta: $\Delta = N(d_1)$ for Call, $N(d_1) - 1$ for Put.
• Gamma: $\Gamma = \frac{N'(d_1)}{S \sigma \sqrt{T}}$ (Curvature & dynamic hedge frequency).
• Vega: $\mathcal{V} = S \sqrt{T} N'(d_1)$ (Sensitivity to 1% IV shift).
• Theta: Time decay per calendar day.
2. Risk Limits & Portfolio Optimization:
• Sharpe Ratio: $\frac{R_p - R_f}{\sigma_p}$ annualized using $\sqrt{252}$.
• Sortino Ratio: Measures return against semi-variance / downside risk only.
• Parametric & Historical VaR: Maximum expected loss at 95% and 99% confidence intervals.
• Kelly Criterion: $f^* = p - \frac{1-p}{b}$ for position sizing.
